User Personas

Project: COSMOS – Cognitive Orchestration for Smart Market Operating System

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

---

Overview

This document defines the primary users of COSMOS. Every feature, workflow, AI recommendation, and interface should be designed to solve the needs of these personas.

---

Persona 1 – Retail Forex Trader

Profile

- Beginner to Intermediate
- Trades Forex through MT5
- Limited technical knowledge
- Trades part-time

Goals

- Find high-quality trade opportunities
- Reduce losses
- Improve trading discipline
- Learn from AI explanations

Challenges

- Emotional trading
- Poor risk management
- Too many indicators
- Lack of confidence

Cosmos Solution

- AI market analysis
- Risk management guidance
- Trade Intelligence Workspace
- Explainable trade recommendations

---

Persona 2 – Crypto Trader

Profile

- Intermediate to Advanced
- Trades multiple cryptocurrencies
- Active throughout the day

Goals

- Monitor multiple markets
- Receive real-time AI insights
- Execute trades efficiently
- Track portfolio performance

Challenges

- High market volatility
- Information overload
- Missed opportunities

Cosmos Solution

- Multi-market monitoring

- Binance connector

Common User Needs

Across all personas, COSMOS should provide:

- Transparent AI reasoning
- Reliable risk management
- Fast market insights
- Simple and intuitive interface
- Secure broker connectivity
- Cross-market support
- Professional trading experience

---

Design Principles

Every feature should answer these questions:

- Does it solve a real user problem?
- Does it reduce complexity?
- Does it improve decision quality?
- Does it support disciplined trading?
- Is it understandable without extensive training?

Approval

This document defines the primary users of COSMOS and will guide all future product, design, engineering, and AI development decisions.
