Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: COSMOS – Cognitive Orchestration for Smart Market Operating System

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Owner: Cosmos Engineering Team

Sprint: Sprint 1 – Foundation

---

1. Executive Summary

COSMOS is an AI-native Trading Operating System designed to help traders analyze markets, make informed decisions, manage risk, and execute trades through a unified cloud platform.

Rather than functioning as a traditional trading bot, COSMOS coordinates multiple AI agents, market data, and broker connectors to deliver transparent, explainable, and risk-aware trading assistance across multiple financial markets.

---

2. Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI-native trading platform that combines intelligent market analysis, transparent decision-making, and universal broker connectivity.

---

3. Mission

Empower traders with AI that explains every recommendation, prioritizes risk management, and integrates seamlessly with multiple brokers and financial markets.

---

4. Problem Statement

Modern traders often rely on disconnected tools for charting, news, execution, portfolio management, and risk analysis.

This leads to:

- Information overload
- Emotional decision-making
- Poor risk management
- Fragmented workflows
- Limited transparency in automated systems

COSMOS addresses these problems by providing one intelligent platform where AI assists users throughout the trading lifecycle.

---

5. Objectives

The first version of COSMOS aims to:

- Deliver AI-assisted market analysis.
- Support multiple financial markets.
- Connect with external brokers.
- Explain every trading decision.
- Centralize portfolio management.
- Improve consistency in risk management.

---

6. Target Users

Primary users:

- Retail traders
- Professional traders
- Prop firm traders
- Crypto traders
- Forex traders

Future users:

- Investment teams
- Trading educators
- Asset managers
- Financial institutions

---

7. Product Scope (Version 1)

Included:

- Web dashboard
- AI assistant
- Multi-agent analysis
- MT5 connector
- Binance connector
- Trade Intelligence Workspace
- Portfolio dashboard
- Risk engine
- User authentication
- Notification system

Excluded:

- Social trading
- Copy trading
- Strategy marketplace
- Mobile application
- Plugin marketplace

---

8. Success Metrics

The platform should achieve:

- Reliable AI-assisted decision support
- Stable broker connectivity
- Transparent AI reasoning
- High system availability
- Secure user authentication
- Positive user feedback on usability

---

9. Product Principles

Every feature in COSMOS must follow these principles:

1. Transparency over mystery.
2. Safety before profitability.
3. Cloud-first architecture.
4. Modular system design.
5. User control over automation.
6. Explainable AI decisions.
7. Security by design.
8. Scalability from day one.
