User Journey

Project: COSMOS – Cognitive Orchestration for Smart Market Operating System

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

---

Overview

This document describes the complete user journey within the COSMOS platform, from account creation to post-trade analysis.

The objective is to ensure a smooth, intelligent, and transparent experience at every stage.

---

Stage 1 – Registration

User Actions

- Create an account
- Verify email
- Complete profile
- Select preferred markets

COSMOS Responsibilities

- Secure authentication
- User profile creation
- Default workspace setup

---

Stage 2 – Connect Broker

User Actions

- Select broker or exchange
- Enter API credentials or account details
- Authorize connection

Supported platforms include:

- MT5
- Binance
- Indian brokers (future)
- Additional connectors

COSMOS Responsibilities

- Validate credentials
- Test connection
- Securely store encrypted credentials
- Display connection status

---

Stage 3 – Dashboard

After login, users arrive at the main dashboard.

Dashboard includes:

- Portfolio summary
- Running trades
- AI Assistant
- Market overview
- News & events
- Risk summary
- Account health

---

Stage 4 – Market Analysis

Users can select a market or instrument.

COSMOS activates multiple AI agents to analyze:

- Technical structure
- Liquidity
- Market sentiment
- News
- Trend
- Risk
- Volatility

The results are combined into a unified market assessment.

---

Stage 5 – Trade Intelligence Workspace

Selecting a trading opportunity opens the Trade Intelligence Workspace.

This workspace displays:

- Interactive chart
- AI reasoning
- Entry price
- Stop loss
- Take profit
- Confidence score
- Risk analysis
- Supporting evidence
- Trade timeline
- Manual controls

Users may:

- Accept
- Reject
- Modify
- Save
- Monitor

the proposed trade.

---

Stage 6 – Trade Execution

Execution options include:

- Manual execution
- Semi-automatic execution
- Future fully automated workflows

COSMOS validates:

- Risk limits
- Account status
- Position sizing
- Broker availability

before sending any order.

---

Stage 7 – Live Monitoring

After execution, users monitor:

- Live P/L
- Position details
- AI updates
- Market changes
- Risk exposure

COSMOS continues evaluating the trade until closure.

---

Stage 8 – Trade Completion

When the trade closes, COSMOS records:

- Entry
- Exit
- Profit/Loss
- Duration
- AI reasoning
- User modifications
- Market conditions

---

Stage 9 – Trade Review

Users receive a complete post-trade report.

The report includes:

- Performance summary
- AI evaluation
- Risk assessment
- Lessons learned
- Improvement suggestions

---

Stage 10 – Continuous Learning

COSMOS uses completed trades to:

- Improve future recommendations
- Personalize AI assistance
- Identify recurring user behavior
- Enhance overall decision quality

---

User Experience Principles

The platform should always be:

- Transparent
- Fast
- Secure
- Explainable
- Consistent
- Professional
- Easy to understand
